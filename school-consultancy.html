Common questions

When do admissions start?

Most schools open registration for the next session between October and February.

Which documents are needed?

Usually a birth certificate, address proof, previous report cards, a transfer certificate and photos.

Do you guarantee admission?

No. Admission is the school’s decision. We improve your chances by matching and preparing well.
